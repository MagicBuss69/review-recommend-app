# 🔥 Engagement ideas

Goal: give people a reason to come back and keep using BiteBuddy.
These are **Phase 5 (Polish & money)** — only matter once a crowd is using the app.

## 1. Streaks that DON'T fade 🔥
Magic's idea: like Snapchat streaks, but they never disappear.

- A normal streak fades on purpose — fear of losing it makes people return daily. If it
  never fades, it becomes more like a **total score / collection of milestones**.
- Fits BiteBuddy: never-fading = friendly, no guilt if you miss a day. Matches the warm
  "Friendly & Fun" vibe.
- **Plan:** have BOTH — **Milestones (never fade):** "Tried 10 / 50 / 100 restaurants."
  **Optional streak (can fade):** for people who like the pressure.
- **Streak window = weekly, NOT daily (Magic's idea):** nobody tries a new restaurant
  every day, so a daily streak is impossible and makes people quit. Use a longer window —
  visit/use within 7–10 days to keep it. A streak should match how often people NATURALLY
  do the thing. Could even let the user pick their pace.

## 2. Leaderboard 🏆
Rank users so they can compete.

- **Big decision:** rank people on WHAT? Whatever we rank becomes what people do more of.
  - Most restaurants reviewed/looked up.
  - Most *helpful* tips (others vote them up) — rewards quality, not spam. ⭐ recommended.
  - Best taste-match guesses.
- **Safety:** public leaderboards + a 13-year-old's app = mind kids' privacy (nicknames,
  not real names). See COPPA note in CLAUDE.md.

## 3. Prizes 🎁
Reward top users.

- **Cost note:** real prizes cost money and have legal rules (contests have laws).
  Anything with money/contracts → a parent/sibling handles it (Magic is 13, the builder).
- **Free version first:** digital rewards — badges, titles ("Top Reviewer this week"), a
  homepage spotlight. Cheap, safe, motivating.
- **Real prizes:** only once the app earns money. No promises.

## 4. Points → profile customization 💎 (the "economy")
Magic's idea: earn points, then spend them to customize your profile — banners, badges,
colors, etc.

- **Why it's the glue:** points need something to SPEND on or they get boring.
  Customization gives points a purpose. This earn-and-spend loop is a game **"economy."**
- **Perfect for a cheap app:** a banner/badge is just a picture — costs us almost nothing,
  but people love showing off. Motivates WITHOUT costing money (unlike real prizes).
- **Earn points by:** reviewing places, helpful tips voted up, streaks, milestones.
- **Spend on:** banners, badge/avatar styles, name colors, custom titles. Some cheap,
  some rare/expensive = a goal to grind.
- **Later money idea:** a few extra-fancy cosmetics could be the paid upgrade — people
  fund the app by buying looks, not by paying to use it.

## 5. EXCLUSIVE cosmetics for top contenders 👑 (the "prestige" reward)
Magic's idea: top people on the leaderboard get custom looks NOBODY else can get — not
buyable with points, only won.

- **Why powerful:** if anyone can buy a banner, it's nice but not special. A banner you
  can ONLY get by being #1 = proof you were the best. People compete hard for things money
  can't buy. This is **exclusivity / prestige.**
- **Make it time-limited:** e.g. "Top Reviewer — June 2026." People return each month to
  win or defend it, instead of winning once and leaving.
- **Tiers so more people feel special:** top 1 / top 10 / top 100 each get their own look.
- **Safety:** still use nicknames on the public leaderboard (kids' privacy / COPPA).
