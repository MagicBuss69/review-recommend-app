BiteBuddy — Mascot / Logo Concept  🐾🍴
========================================

Chosen direction (2026-06-06): "Forky Critter"
A little friendly creature whose body is a fork. Quirky + memorable.

Rough sketch:

          ^   ^
         ( o   o )      <- big friendly eyes
          \  ^  /       <- tiny smile
        ___|||___
           |||          <- body is a fork
           |||
          _|||_

How the REAL (colored) version should look:
- Body / fork: BLUE  (main brand color = trust)  💙
- Cheeks / a little leaf or "fresh" accent: GREEN (food/fresh/good) 💚
- Background: white / transparent (clean, friendly)
- Style: rounded, soft edges, smiling — Friendly & Fun, not sharp or scary
- Should still look good TINY (as a little website icon / "favicon")

Ideas for later:
- The fork "prongs" could double as a little crown or hair = extra cute
- A happy version + a "thinking" version (for when the app is loading a review)
- Holding a tiny magnifying glass = "checking the reviews for you"
