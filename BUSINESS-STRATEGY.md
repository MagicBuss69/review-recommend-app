# 📈 BiteBuddy — Business Plan

How BiteBuddy can grow from a fun project into a real little business.
Written for Magic (the founder 🎩). Keep it simple, grow one step at a time.

---

## 🎯 1. The big idea (in one line)

> Paste a restaurant link → get honest reviews + an **AI waiter** + a pick made for *you*.

**Why people would use it:** picking where to eat is annoying. Reviews are messy and
fake-y, and you never know if *you'll* like it. BiteBuddy does the reading for you and
makes it personal. The **AI waiter (Tony)** is the "wow" nobody else has. 🍽️

---

## 🎯 1.5 Why BiteBuddy beats free AI (our positioning)

The danger: an "AI wrapper" that just relays AI answers is pointless (people would
use free AI directly). So the AI is only ONE ingredient — the product is **trusted,
personal, instant local food decisions.**

**Main headline (chosen 2026-06-07): "AI guesses. BiteBuddy knows." 🐾**
Lead with **real, current, verified LOCAL data — no hallucination.** Free AI makes
things up about small local places; BiteBuddy pulls real map/review data and tells the
AI "only use this, invent nothing."

Backed up by our other edges: it **remembers your taste**, it's **one tap** (no prompting),
it's **visual** (map, photos, AI waiter), it's **honest** (declines unverified places),
and it builds **community review data only we have.**

**The rule that protects the brand:** ALWAYS ground answers in real data, or say "I don't
know." One made-up answer cracks the "honest one" promise. Show trust signals like
"Based on N real reviews" / "✅ Verified place."

## 👥 2. Who it's for (our "customers")

- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Everyday people deciding where to eat tonight
- ✈️ Travelers / tourists in a new city who don't know the good spots
- 🍕 Food lovers who eat out a lot (these become our biggest fans)

Start with **food places**, because that's where BiteBuddy is most useful and special.

---

## 💰 3. How BiteBuddy makes money

Don't add these on day 1 — add them once people actually use and love it.

| Way | How it works | When to add |
|-----|-------------|-------------|
| 🆓→⭐ **Free + Premium** | Basic reviews free. Pay a small monthly fee for unlimited checks, saved favorites, and the full AI waiter. | After people love the free version |
| 📣 **Featured restaurants** | Restaurants pay a little to be highlighted (clearly marked as an ad, to stay honest). | Once you have lots of users |
| 🔗 **Booking / order links** | "Book a table" or "Order now" buttons that pay BiteBuddy a small cut. | Mid-stage |

**Golden rule:** the people who use it the MOST are the most likely to pay — so a generous
free version that turns superfans into paying members is the main money engine. 💚
(Money/accounts need a parent's help since Magic is 13 — Magic builds, a grown-up owns.)

---

## 📍 3.5 Launch city: LANDSKRONA, Sweden 🇸🇪 (decided 2026-06-07)

Start in Magic's home town — Landskrona, Skåne. Smart because:
- 🏠 **Home advantage** — Magic lives there; can visit restaurants & knows the scene.
- 🤏 **Small enough to OWN** — realistic to cover *every* restaurant in town and become
  "the app that has all of Landskrona". (Impossible in a huge city.)
- 🆕 **Newly-opened restaurants = the hook** — new places want attention; locals are
  curious. BiteBuddy helps both. Lead marketing with "the new spots in Landskrona".
- ⛴️ **Some tourists** (Ven island ferry passes through) → reason to support English too.

**Language:** reviews are mostly in Swedish; support Swedish + English (for tourists).

**Expansion ladder:** Landskrona → Helsingborg → Malmö + Skåne → rest of Sweden → beyond.
Own one circle before moving to the next.

**First mission:** (1) list Landskrona's restaurants, (2) highlight newly-opened ones,
(3) show 10 local friends/family, (4) improve from their feedback.

## 🧲 4. Getting the FIRST users (the real challenge)

Remember: the #1 thing that kills new apps is **too few users**, not too many.
So this is the most important page. Goal: get the **first 10**, then the first 100.

**Cheap, beginner-friendly ways to get started:**
1. 👨‍👩‍👧 **Friends & family first** — show 10 real people, watch them use it, ask "would you use this?"
2. 🏫 **School & word of mouth** — classmates, food-loving friends, family group chats
2. 📱 **Short videos** (TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Reels) — show Tony the AI waiter answering
   funny questions. The AI waiter is *very* shareable. 🎥
4. 🍴 **Local angle** — pick ONE town/city and become the best for restaurants there first
5. 💬 **Listen** — use the feedback box! Early users tell you exactly what to fix.

> Tiny goal that beats everything: **make 10 real people actually use it and like it.**

---

## 📣 5. Marketing a 13-year-old can really do

- 🎬 Make short, fun clips of Tony the AI waiter (people love AI doing surprising things)
- 🐾 Let Forky have personality online — a friendly mascot is memorable
- 🗣️ Tell a clear story: "I'm 13 and I built this to help people pick where to eat"
  (people *love* rooting for a young founder — that's a real advantage!)
- 🆓 Keep it free + easy to try (no sign-up wall at first) so sharing is frictionless
- 🤝 Ask happy users to share it with one friend

---

## 🪜 6. Growth ladder (how far it can go)

| Stage | What it looks like | Goal |
|-------|-------------------|------|
| 1 | You + friends use it | "It works!" |
| 2 | 100 strangers use it | "People like it" |
| 3 | First $1 earned 💵 | "It's a real business" |
| 4 | Thousands of users, small income | A side business |
| 5 | Real company, maybe a small team | The dream 🌟 |

Ways to expand later: more place types (cafés → shops → hotels), more cities/countries,
a phone-app version, and a smarter AI waiter for every restaurant.

---

## 💵 7. Costs to watch (stay cheap!)

- 🤖 AI usage (Gemini has a free tier to start — great!)
- 🗺️ Review data API (needs a card on file even for free use → parent's help)
- ☁️ Servers (cheap/free while small; auto-scale only when crowds come)
- 🧠 **Caching** keeps costs low: popular places get analyzed once, then reused.

Spend almost nothing until people show up. Low cost = low risk = you can't really "lose."

---

## ⚠️ 8. Honest risks (and the plan)

| Risk | Plan |
|------|------|
| Nobody uses it | Start tiny, show real people early, listen & improve |
| Costs grow | Caching + free tiers + spend only when users arrive |
| Legal trouble | Use **official APIs** (no scraping); a grown-up owns the business |
| Burnout | Small evening chunks + breaks (workouts 💪), celebrate small wins |

---

## ✅ The strategy in one sentence

> **Build something small, cheap, and legal that 10 real people love — turn superfans
> into paying members — and grow one city and one step at a time.**
