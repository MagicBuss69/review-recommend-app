About BiteBuddy 🐾

The friendly buddy who reads the reviews so you don't have to.

🍴 What is BiteBuddy?

Type a restaurant's name and Forky reads its reviews, sums up the good and the not-so-good, and gives you a pick made for YOUR taste. Less scrolling, more eating.

👦 Who made it?

BiteBuddy is built by Magic — a 13-year-old from Landskrona, Sweden — learning to code by building something real. It starts in Landskrona and grows from there.

🔒 Your privacy

Everything you add — your tastes, notes, and recent searches — is saved ONLY in your own browser. Not on a server, not anywhere we can see.

We don't track you, show ads, or sell your data. Full stop.

When real accounts arrive later, this page will be updated with a full privacy policy, set up with an adult.

💬 Got ideas?

Forky loves feedback! Use the idea box on the home page to tell us what you'd love to see.
